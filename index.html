About Junhao Ye

Hi! I am Junhao Ye (叶俊豪), a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Integrated Circuits at Southeast University, advised by Prof. Zhe Jiang. I received my B.S. in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from Shenzhen University, ranking first in my major and earning a recommendation for graduate study at Southeast University.

My research focuses on AI for Electronic Design Automation (EDA), with an emphasis on LLM-aided RTL verification: UVM testbench generation, assertion generation, and RTL security analysis.

I have four papers at the leading EDA conferences ICCAD and DAC. I am also a research intern at the National Center for Technology Innovation in EDA, where some of my research work has been integrated into ChatDV to support end-to-end automated UVM verification for small IPs. I have received the National Scholarship, Excellent Star of SZU, and Jicui Graduate recognition.

Research Intern 实习经历

RESEARCH INTERN2025.07 - Present

National Center for Technology Innovation in EDA

Worked on LLM-aided UVM testbench generation, decomposing IP-level verification into component-wise generation, iterative error checking, repair, and stimulus enhancement. Built and evaluated a workflow that connects RTL, specifications, protocol context, simulation feedback, and debugging into one usable verification process.

The resulting workflow was integrated as a verification tool in ChatDV (project note), enabling small IP-level UVM verification.
